## About Me

I'm a **BS Computer Science** student at the **University of the Philippines Baguio** *(He/Him)*. Outside class, I'm an apprentice at the **Machine Intelligence and Neural Dynamics Laboratory (MINDLab) – UP Baguio** and a peer mentor at the **Learning Resource Center (LRC) – UP Baguio**. I like joining hackathons and conferences, and working with people to turn ideas into working products.

- **Interests:** Cybersecurity & Cryptography, Quantum Computing, Data Science & Data Engineering
- **Currently learning:** Post-Quantum Cryptography, preparing for a Cybersecurity certification, and practicing Data Science & Data Engineering fundamentals
- **Outside tech:** Reading, hiking, and having good conversations with people
- **Fun fact:** Shifted from **BS Mathematics** to **BS Computer Science** thinking I could escape math, and CS quickly said *"nope."* Still enjoying CS a lot more because I get to see how it all gets built and applied.
- **Open to:** Work, internships, apprenticeships, and collaborations — feel free to look around my repos or reach out if you have an idea you want to build together.
